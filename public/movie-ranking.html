My Movie Project

My favourite movies in the year 2025

My Fault London

This was an awesome movie. The chemistry was so good between the leads and the guy was a real man. The plot and screenplay was also better than the original

Blue Lock

This was a great anime. I unexpectedly loved it even though it was about soccer. The plot was really good and it was truly inspirational in some ways.

Apothecary Diaries

A fun and light hearted anime with a lot of fun characters. I love the personality of the female lead and her idgaf attitude.
